1Welcome and installing

STRATUM is a music production app for the Mac. It plays and records audio, runs software instruments and effects — its own, and the Audio Unit, VST3 and VST2 plugins installed on your Mac — and opens projects from FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro and Reason.

Installing

  1. Open STRATUM.dmg and drag STRATUM into Applications.
  2. Open STRATUM. Because this alpha is not yet notarised by Apple, macOS stops it the first time.
  3. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway next to STRATUM. You only do this once.

The first time you open it

STRATUM opens with a short demo song and asks whether you want a tour. The tour highlights each part of the window and lets you try Play and the sound library as you go. You can take it again any time from Help › Take the Tour.

Permissions STRATUM may ask for

  • Microphone — only when you first record.
  • Photos — only when you share to an Instagram Story, so the video can reach your phone.
  • Camera — only when you go live with your camera on.
  • Screen & System Audio Recording — when you record from another app, or go live, so viewers hear your sound.

2The window at a glance

6STRATUM Wave

STRATUM Wave is a wavetable synthesizer, in the same family as Serum, Vital and Pigments. A wavetable is a series of single waveforms; the Position control sweeps through them, which is what makes wavetable sounds move. Choose Wavetable synth (STRATUM Wave) in a track's Instrument menu.

Mod envelope and LFO can move the filter cutoff and the wavetable position; the LFO can follow the song tempo.

SectionControls

For a wobble bass, load the Wobble Bass preset and change LFO › Sync between 1/4, 1/8 and 1/16 to change the wobble speed in time with the song.

7Writing notes and patterns

  • Step grid (Pattern skin): click squares in the Channel Rack to place drum hits on sixteenth notes.
  • Piano Roll: click to add a note, drag to move it, drag its right edge to change its length, Option-click to delete. Scale lock keeps new notes in the song's key.
  • Session players: in the Piano Roll's Session player menu choose Drummer, Bass, Chords or Arpeggio and press Regenerate — STRATUM writes a part that follows the scene's chords (the Chords box, in scale degrees, e.g. 1 6 3 7).
  • Keys: turn on Keys in the top bar to play the selected track from the computer keyboard (A–L row; Z and X change octave).
  • Dump Score Log: STRATUM keeps the last five minutes of everything you played, recording or not. Press Dump Score Log to put it into the selected clip.

8Arranging your song

STRATUM has two ways of playing, chosen with the Scene loop / Song menu:

  • Scene loop repeats one scene — a pattern or a set of clips — while you work on it, like FL Studio's pattern mode or an Ableton scene.
  • Song plays the arrangement: regions placed freely on each track's timeline, with named sections across the top.

In the timeline, drag regions to move them, drag their right edge to lengthen or loop them, ⌘E splits at the playhead and ⌘D duplicates. Section buttons move, duplicate or delete whole sections.

Automation: volume and pan automation imported from other software plays in Song mode; the Inspector shows a track's automation and can clear it. Drawing your own automation is planned for a later version.

9Recording and comping

  1. Add an audio track and press its arm button (or just press Record — STRATUM arms or adds an audio track for you).
  2. Turn on Monitor if you want to hear yourself; this is direct monitoring, without effects.
  3. Press Record. Each pass over the same place becomes a new take.
  4. Comp the best parts: in the take lanes below a recorded region, drag across a take to use that part of it.

STRATUM removes the audio interface's delay from recordings automatically, so takes line up with the beat.

10Effects

Effects are added in the Devices panel. STRATUM's own effects run in its audio engine; plugin effects run in the Mac's plugin engine.

The signal path of a track. A track with any plugin on it — including STRATUM Wave, STRATUM Tune and Airwindows — plays through the plugin engine instead and skips STRATUM's own effects and the master section.

Prompt-to-Plugin

Click + Prompt-to-Plugin and describe the effect you want in plain words — “dusty lo-fi tape with a little room”, “sidechain pump”, “stutter”. STRATUM builds a chain of effects to match and shows a knob for each setting.

Airwindows

508 Airwindows effects are built in — reverbs, tape, saturation, console emulation, EQs, compressors, clippers and more. Choose + Plugin effect…; Airwindows effects are listed with their category, and the recommended ones come first. Their controls appear in the Devices panel.

STRATUM Tune

Pitch correction for vocals and other single-note parts. It starts in the song's key and scale; Retune speed runs from instant (the robotic effect) to natural, with Amount and Mix.

Back panel and LFOs

Press Tab to flip the Devices panel round. Patch an LFO's CV output into an effect's input to move that setting in time — like the back of a Reason rack.

11The sound library

Click Sounds to browse 791 free sounds — kicks, snares, claps, hats, cymbals, toms, percussion, found sounds, vocal shouts and vocal percussion — and 99 MIDI chord, bass and piano kits. They are public domain (CC0): use them in anything, with no credit needed.

Record from another app

Choose File › Record from Another App… or the button at the top of the Sounds browser. Pick the app (a web browser, a music player), choose where the sound goes — a new audio track, the sampler or a drum pad — press Record and play the sound in that app. Only that app is recorded, and silence at both ends is trimmed. The first time, macOS asks you to allow STRATUM under Privacy & Security › Screen & System Audio Recording. Only record sounds you have the right to use.

12Mixing

Each track has a volume fader, pan knob, mute (M) and solo (S). The Master strip has Drive (gentle console-style saturation) and Glue (bus compression). Each effect chain and the master end in a limiter, so STRATUM's own output doesn't clip.

13Stems: splitting a track apart

Select an audio clip and press Separate stems (AI) in its clip settings. STRATUM separates the audio into drums, bass, vocals and other instruments on four new tracks, using the HT-Demucs model on your Mac — nothing is uploaded. This needs an Apple silicon Mac and can take about as long as the audio itself.

Going live

The Go live row in the Share window streams your session as you work. Viewers see the STRATUM window and, if you tick Show my camera in a box, your camera in a small box beside the window (the window shrinks a little to make room, so nothing is covered). They hear whatever your Mac plays, plus your microphone if you add it. Pick a platform, paste the server URL and stream key it gives you, and press Go live. A red ● LIVE timer appears at the top of the window. Press it to end the stream.

Tick Remember for next time to keep the key in your Mac's Keychain. Keep your stream key private: anyone who has it can stream to your channel. If you stream with OBS today, STRATUM does that job on its own, sending 720p video at 30 frames a second.

17Plugins

STRATUM finds the Audio Unit, VST3 and VST2 plugins installed on your Mac. Choose Plugin… in a track's Instrument menu for an instrument, or + Plugin effect… for an effect on any track.

  • Open plugin window shows the plugin's own controls; Controls shows its settings inside STRATUM's Devices panel.
  • Plugin settings are saved in the project and included in exports.
  • Plugins receive the song's tempo and position, so tempo-synced plugins follow the song.
  • VST3 plugins are looked for in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3 and ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3; VST2 in the matching VST folders.

Plugins run inside STRATUM, so a faulty plugin can close the app. Save often when trying a new plugin.

18Limits in this alpha and troubleshooting

  • “STRATUM can't be opened” — use System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway (Chapter 1).
  • Stem separation needs an Apple silicon Mac.
  • Many older VST2 plugins are Intel-only and won't load on Apple silicon.
  • Imported projects bring notes, arrangement and samples, but not other software's own instruments, mixer effects or most automation.
  • STRATUM can't post to social platforms by itself; it opens each upload page with your video ready to drag in.
  • Automation can be played and cleared but not yet drawn.
