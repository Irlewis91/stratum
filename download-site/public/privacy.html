Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This policy explains what Matova LC ("Matova", "we") collects when you use STRATUM and this website, why, and the choices you have. The short version: your music stays on your device unless you choose to sync, share or stream it, and we do not sell your personal information.

What stays on your device

Projects, recordings, My Sounds and settings are kept on your device unless you use Sync. Live-stream keys you choose to save are kept in your device's secure storage (the Keychain on Mac). Camera, microphone and app-audio input are used only when you start recording or streaming, and we do not receive them. STRATUM has no advertising and no third-party analytics.

What goes to other services

  • Vercel hosts this website. GitHub hosts the downloads and the web app.
  • Discord handles sign-in, under Discord's own privacy policy.
  • Supabase stores Sync accounts and projects, if you use Sync.
  • GitHub and the Internet Archive serve sounds from the optional sound packs. The first time you use one of those sounds, your device requests it directly from them, so they see your IP address.
  • Platforms you choose, such as Instagram, YouTube or Twitch, receive what you share or stream to them, under their own terms.

We do not sell personal information or share it for targeted advertising. We may disclose information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a business transfer, in which case this policy continues to apply.

How long we keep it

The sign-in cookie lasts up to one day, or until you sign out. Host logs are kept for the short period our providers set. Sync data stays until you delete it or your account, and then leaves backups as they expire. Email is kept only as long as needed to help you, or as the law requires.

Your choices and rights

You can sign out, stop using Sync, and delete synced projects at any time. Depending on where you live, including California and the EU, UK and EEA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or get a copy of your personal information, to object to or limit some uses, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. To make a request, email ian@matovagroup.com. We may need to confirm it is you.

If you are in the EU, UK or EEA: we rely on performing our agreement with you for the Service, on our legitimate interest in keeping it secure, and on your consent where we ask for it. Your information may be processed in the United States, under the safeguards our providers offer.

Children

STRATUM is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email ian@matovagroup.com and we will delete it.

Security

We protect data with encryption in transit, signed session cookies, per-user access rules on Sync data, and security headers. No service is perfectly secure, so please use a safe email account for Sync.

Changes

If we make a material change we will update this page and the date above, and give notice in the app or on the site where appropriate.
