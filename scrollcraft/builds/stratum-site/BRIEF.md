# STRATUM site: scroll brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation ("use your judgement for scroll craft"). No user quotes are invented here.

Scope: added to the existing landing page, not a rebuild. Real screenshots only, no generated imagery (no API key, no ffmpeg).

1. Vibe: a studio at night, warm amber on near black. References: an arrangement window, a VU meter, a late session.
2. Journey: what it is, one studio that becomes any studio, what it does, your old sessions come too, why, join and download.
3. Energy: calm open, building through the hero, loud at the layouts, calm through features, quiet before the close, one held landing.
4. Feeling curve:
   - Hero: curiosity. The screenshot tilts up and a playhead starts when you move.
   - Layouts: delight (the peak). The same studio rewrites itself under your hand.
   - Features: confidence. Plain cards, nothing moving but their arrival.
   - Why and FAQ: trust. Still on purpose (authored silence).
   - Download: resolution. The bars land on a final chord and hold.
5. Peak: "you scroll and the whole studio turns into a different one, five times". Lives in the layouts act, which has the largest span.
6. Tell-someone: It's the site where scrolling plays the song.
7. Range: stays premium dark, the brand already exists.
8. World: distinct scenes, not one continuous world.
9. Assets: real STRATUM screenshots already on the site.

Grammar: Playback, a new one. Sections are parts of a song and the visitor's hand is the transport. Bans: progress readouts, section counters, a pinned type act, a card rail, autoplay audio, motion that runs without the visitor (bars rest until scrolled).

Signature move: scroll velocity is the signal. The equaliser only moves while you scroll, and a playhead is the same object in the hero sweep and in the layout wipe.

Score:
| Beat | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | pin + parallax, layered planes | Depth, and the playhead appears under their hand |
| Turn (peak) | pin + reveal wipe | A wipe is a change of state, which is what the layouts are |
| Substance | flow + in | Reading needs stillness |
| Commitment | kinetic bars landing | The song ends, the page resolves |

Engine: the bundled engine is not used. It is built around scrub video and this page has none, so a small script drives CSS variables instead.
